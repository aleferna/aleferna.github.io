*Example post. Replace or delete it.*

1. **First pass:** title, abstract, figures, and conclusion. Decide whether it is worth more time.
2. **Second pass:** read the body, skipping proofs and derivations, and note the key claims.
3. **Third pass:** work through the details as if re-deriving the paper yourself.

> The goal of the first pass is to decide, not to understand.
